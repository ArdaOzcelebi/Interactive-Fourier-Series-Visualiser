8 · How this tool computes

Integrals are evaluated numerically, piece by piece, with 8-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature. The pieces are split automatically at every jump or corner (inline piecewise conditions, |·|, u(·), rect, floor …), so the results are accurate to about 10⁻¹². All 500 harmonics are computed at once using a trigonometric recurrence.

When the coefficients follow a recognisable pattern (for example for odd n), the exact formula is detected and shown with an exact tag.
